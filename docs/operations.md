# Operating the service

The premium path is a responsive conversation and a trustworthy debrief. During an outage, **preserve accepted answers and confirmed facts first**; stop discovery, optional evaluations and speech before losing or inventing candidate work. Targets below are proposed launch gates, not measured performance or SLAs.

### Authentication, privacy and authorization

- For the [customer sign-in flow](architecture.md#customer-sign-in), Entra External ID authenticates candidates; the app owns sessions and authorization, never passwords. Use separate staging and production registrations, exact HTTPS callbacks, scoped secrets and renewal alerts. Protect login and mutations against CSRF, enforce origin checks, and revoke sessions on logout or account disablement. Require fresh authentication for linking, export-all, deletion and billing changes. Do not merge accounts by matching email strings. Linking a LinkedIn profile is separate from login.
- Derive candidate scope from the verified subject on every API call, stream, upload/download grant and tool invocation. Use parameterized queries, transaction-local tenant scope with pooled connections, `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`, non-owner application roles without bypass, and composite tenant FKs. A worker rehydrates the owner from an authorized run; queue payloads and prompts cannot choose it. A narrow dispatcher can claim work across tenants but cannot read resumes. Test with two candidates who have identically named projects.
- Encrypt transport, DB, blobs and backups. Grant private, short-lived object access after a fresh ownership check. Separate upload quarantine, clean documents, and export access. Restrict outbound fetches to approved sources; check resolved destinations and redirects, size, type, time and source rights. Treat JDs, documents, web text and model output as untrusted. Do not let a document trigger a tool action, arbitrary network access, or billing change.
- Treat reviews, referrals and project links as private evidence, not company research. Ask permission to process the material, allow passage selection/redaction before model access, and exclude referrers' contact details. Do not contact the author, send private project URLs to search, ingest access tokens, or put excerpts in a cross-user cache.
- Send only the minimum required data to contracted processors with appropriate region, retention, deletion and no-training terms. Strip contact details from model packets; insert them locally into approved exports. Only sanitized public company/role terms reach a search provider. Keep raw audio, prompts, transcripts, resume text, tool arguments and signed URLs out of analytics, error reports and logs. Don't send private documents to general consumer chat products. No employer access, candidate ranking for employers, hidden live assistance, or voice biometrics.
- The candidate can export their structured library and artifacts, disable session recall, revoke email/voice/research permissions, or delete their account. Cancel active work, revoke sessions and prevent queued messages resurrecting a deleted profile. See the [retention matrix](data-model.md#retention-contract-proposed-for-the-pilot). Use just-in-time, MFA-protected, audited support access; default console shows IDs and state only. No transcript lookup by ordinary operators. Abuse limits apply per verified account and origin without making audio or disclosure of sensitive information mandatory.
- A live guide starts with **mic off**, even after a voice mock. Notes must be permitted; adaptive listening also needs fresh, explicit permission from everyone whose speech could be heard. Enable it only when on-device processing is available and verified: no meeting audio, transcript, inferred topic, or question goes to a server, analytics or an evaluator. Revocation stops capture immediately. A separate feature flag disables adaptive cues without disabling manual cards or practice voice.

### Billing and unit economics

**Tier structure, not a price decision:** offer a free experience and an optional monthly Prep subscription. The paid amount is **TBD**. Do not create a live Stripe Price or open Checkout until pilot cost measurements and willingness-to-pay research support a decision. No card to try, auto-conversion, auto-top-up or usage overage. Proposed allowances below are pilot hypotheses.

| Offer | What the candidate gets | Boundary and reset |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Try the coach** (anonymous, $0) | A resettable, browser-only fictional project, mock excerpt and example debrief. Try selecting the evidence the sample candidate missed. | No private uploads, mic, signup, Stripe customer, paid model or external search. No demo records enter a real profile. |
| **Free** (verified account, $0) | Private editable library, two starter evidence imports, base resume export, prep plan/manual live cards, **20 starter builder turns**, **one full text mock**, the complete grade/debrief, and one ungraded retry of its weakest answer. | Starter grants are once per account, not per month or reinstall. No cloud voice, scheduled scout or tailored packet generation. Existing private materials remain readable/exportable within their normal retention period. |
| **Prep** (monthly, amount TBD) | **8 completed full mocks**, **120 active voice minutes** shared by builder and coach, **100 text builder turns**, opt-in daily matches, and **31 tailored resume/letter pairs** (manual + automatic; at most one automatic pair per local day). Imports subject to published page/OCR limits. | Paid allowances reset at the Stripe billing-period boundary, no rollover. Digests/planning/cards do not consume mock credits. If only voice runs out, switch to text; if drafts run out, still deliver eligible matches. |

**What makes the upgrade worth testing:** the candidate has already seen a real debrief before paying. Prep buys repeated attempts, voice and discovery, not access to the verdict they just earned. Test interest in this bundle against [alternatives](market.md). Once pricing is set, show the charge, applicable tax and renewal terms; use versioned allowlisted Price IDs, honor existing paid periods and disclose changes. Ring membership never changes a bill.

**Payment path:** authenticated checkout request -> server selects an allowlisted price and customer -> Stripe-hosted Checkout -> [signed webhook](https://docs.stripe.com/webhooks) -> durable inbox -> reconcile subscription -> local entitlements. Never trust the browser's success redirect. Verify the signature on raw bytes and dedupe event ID before acknowledgment; handle duplicate/out-of-order events by reading current provider state. Retry from the inbox; periodically reconcile missed events and investigate drift. Customer Portal handles payment details and cancellation. Cancellation at period end preserves paid rights to that date. After a failed renewal, give 72 hours to fix payment: finish accepted work and keep read/export access, but pause new paid generation (interactive and scheduled). If payment is not recovered, downgrade without deleting data. Account deletion cancels future renewal, independent of asynchronous purging.

| [Stripe event or lifecycle step](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/webhooks) | Entitlement effect |
| --- | --- |
| Explicit upgrade; `checkout.session.completed` | Reuse the account's mapped customer and an idempotent checkout request. Link the session to the server-side account. An incomplete payment or success redirect grants **nothing**. |
| `invoice.paid` for the expected subscription/price and service period | Confirm current provider state, then open/renew that paid period **once**. Key the grant by subscription, period and meter, not webhook delivery. Never reset a lifetime free grant on upgrade or downgrade. |
| `invoice.payment_failed`, then recovery | Show the portal, finish accepted work, pause new paid generation and notify once. A later confirmed paid invoice restores the proper period; an older failure cannot revoke a newer paid period. After the grace window, fall back to Free with its remaining grants. |
| `customer.subscription.updated` / `.deleted`; refund or dispute | Reconcile the effective end date/status; stop new paid work when rights end, cancel future paid schedules, keep read/export. Support handles refunds under the published policy; append financial adjustments instead of rewriting settled usage. |

Meter on the server before provider calls, not in the client or the release flags. Separate the Stripe invoice ledger (money), entitlements (what a plan permits), and usage entries (what was consumed). Expose an authenticated usage screen with remaining units, period boundaries, next charge and a direct Portal link. Products, keys, webhook secrets and signing verification are isolated by Stripe test/live mode; staging exercises test clocks and duplicate delivery without making a real charge.

**Metering path:** atomically reserve allowance and estimated spend before a run; reserve the report budget when a mock starts. Settle user units once per durable successful effect, release abandoned reservations by lease expiry, and reconcile actual provider usage separately. Voice units are accepted input/synthesized audio seconds, shown as minutes; exclude idle/paused time. Explain metering before speaking, limit turn length, and stop generating TTS on cancellation where supported. Reserve in small increments and never deduct the same turn twice. Rate-limit repeated starts/abandons separately. Record provider-call attempts, model/price versions, tokens, audio seconds, searches and billing period; a retry can cost the service money without double-charging the user.

**Worked 20-minute cold-target mock.** These are planning inputs against [OpenAI's public API rate card](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing), not an Azure quote, benchmark, or mandatory model choice. Keep `fast`, `assess` and `speech` aliases pinned to evaluated deployments and a versioned price table. Quantities are totals across the session, not per turn.

| Component | Assumed quantity and rate | USD |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Turn loop, extraction and summaries | 24k input + 3k output on a `gpt-4.1-mini` price baseline ($0.40/$1.60 per million) | 0.0144 |
| Assessment and coaching | 8k input + 1.5k output on a `gpt-4.1` price baseline ($2/$8 per million) | 0.0280 |
| Research on cache miss | 2 standard web searches at $0.01 each + two 8k search-content blocks at the mini input rate, excluded from the first row | 0.0264 |
| Candidate speech | 12 min transcription at the `gpt-4o-mini-transcribe` estimate of $0.003/min | 0.0360 |
| Coach speech | 8 min at an **assumed effective** $0.015/min; [mini TTS](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-4o-mini-tts) bills tokens, so measure actual cost per spoken minute | 0.1200 |
| Total | $0.2248 estimated; add 25% headroom and round the budget up | **0.29** |

A cold text mock under the same assumptions costs $0.0688 before headroom; budget $0.09. For a daily shortlist, 10k mini input + 1k output is $0.0056; budget $0.02/run with shared refresh overhead. For a draft pair, 8k/2k strong tokens + 2k/0.5k mini checking is $0.0336; budget $0.05/pair. These estimates assume permitted board access without a paid feed minimum. Don't buy a commercial feed without adding its license to the model.

Include reminder cards with library access: switching cards costs no mock credit or cloud voice minutes. Preparing evidence is the paid inference step: a 5k-input/500-output extraction at the mini rates above is **$0.0028** before parsing, OCR, and storage. Deduplicate within candidate and permission scope by selected-content hash and extractor version; bound pages/OCR per account and show paste as the inexpensive alternative. Track imports in the builder cost estimate, separately from conversational turns.

| --- | --- |
| Typical usage and variable delivery cost | 4 voice mocks x $0.29 + 31 digests x $0.02 + 8 pairs x $0.05 + **$0.75 assumed** for builder, sampled evals, variable storage, mail and telemetry = **$2.93**. |
| Payment costs | For monthly price `P` in USD, an illustrative US domestic card costs `0.036P + 0.30` using [Stripe Payments](https://stripe.com/pricing) and [Billing](https://stripe.com/billing/pricing). Confirm actual rates by country, method and contract before launch. |
| Contribution before shared costs | `P - payment fees - variable delivery cost`. Recalculate using measured typical **and** heavy voice/draft usage; the allowance is not a cost guarantee. |
| Small-team sensitivity | Allocate shared monthly cost `F` over paying subscribers `N` as `F/N`. Include staging and production DB, backups, compute, registry and monitoring. Add free usage, support, refunds and any feed minimum before choosing a price. |

**Free acquisition budget:** propose a $0.25 variable AI/import budget per fully used free activation, including the $0.09 cold text mock and its reserved report. At 1,000 fully used activations that is $250 in delivery cost, independent of the eventual subscription price. Demo traffic has no per-visitor inference bill. Rate-limit signups/imports and reserve the full first mock before starting. If the global free pool is exhausted, pause *new* AI starts with a clear message; don't truncate an accepted report, hide someone's data, or auto-charge them. Track signup, conversion, abuse, refunds and cost per activation separately.

**Cost controls:** shared cache only for rights-cleared public research (keyed by normalized company/role, locale, source hash, policy and TTL); private caches keyed by tenant, purpose and revisions. Refresh official packs on change or after seven days, sooner on rights expiry; verify freshness of open jobs daily. Batch nonurgent work, skip unchanged inputs, cap context, stop after top three matches, and avoid a model call for hard filters. Enforce per-operation, per-user and daily global budgets, with separate capacity for already-promised reports. Track p50/p95 dollars per completed workflow and actual invoice variance, not just average cost/token. If optional spend runs hot, pause batch generation before degrading assessment quality.

### Feedback and model quality

Thumbs up/down can attach to a turn, report item, match or draft. Follow a downvote with optional reasons: *wrong fact*, *unsupported claim*, *unfair score*, *irrelevant*, *stale*, *slow*. Route wrong facts to an editable diff, not a prompt tweak; route unfair-score challenges to a consented review queue. Record input **versions** and the run ID so a corrected model can be compared to the one that answered. A rating is product feedback, not training consent or an instruction to change a grade.

For live cues, offer **helpful / distracting / wrong project** against the prepared card after use, without attaching meeting content. Add synthetic release cases: team praise in a review is not personal ownership; a private referral can't appear in a draft or card without permission; a deleted project can't reappear in an open deck. Exercise topic changes with fixture audio: output must be an approved card ID or silence. Mic access in manual mode, off-device audio in adaptive mode, and a cue entering a scored mock are release blockers.

| Lane | What it checks | Proposed release or live action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offline deterministic, every PR | Synthetic dual-tenant fixtures, source ownership, valid turn/fact citations, unsupported numbers, rubric math, no hints in scored mode, deletion, quota/replay, DST and duplicate webhook effects. Include deliberately bad outputs. | Any tenant leak, fabricated credential accepted for export, or duplicate debit blocks the release. Replay tests the orchestration, **not** model usefulness. |
| Live comparison on prompt/model/tool changes | Fixed synthetic or licensed fixture set with held-out cases; run baseline/candidate blind, record model, latency, cost and failure slices. Include no JD, conflicting facts, qualitative impact, interrupted audio and unavailable sources. | Pilot gates: >=95% supported draft claims on human-labeled samples, zero invented credentials, >=85% verdict agreement with consensus of two calibrated reviewers on adequately covered mocks. Track reviewer disagreement; hold rollout if a critical slice regresses, even if means improve. |
| Online, all runs | Schema/reference checks, cross-owner checks, coverage required for verdict, rounding/floor rules, freshness, invalidated facts and unsupported-claim blockers before export. | Fail closed on an unsafe claim or isolation error, not on the whole candidate library. Ask for evidence, omit the claim, or mark the report pending. |
| Online, sampled | With separate quality-review consent, redact and sample up to 1% of eligible completed reports/drafts under a daily spend cap. A distinct judge scores grounding, missed examples and actionability; blinded human reviewers calibrate it and examine negative feedback. | Compare each deployed prompt/model version to its prior baseline with denominators and uncertainty. Roll back on material grounding/grade drift. Never promote model-written feedback or private sessions wholesale into a training/eval corpus. |

Pair equivalent written and spoken answers with the **same accepted transcript**; compare verdicts and error rates across supported role/level and modality, including noisy speech and assistive input. Don't infer demographic labels from voice. Report insufficient sample sizes; a thumbs-up rate can reward flattering graders. Pair it with evidence validity, repeat-attempt improvement under the same rubric, second-session completion and draft correction rate.

### Observability, alerts and livesite

Propagate `trace_id`, `operation_id`, `run_id` and queue correlation through HTTP, model/tool calls, DB commit, billing and delivery. Emit structured allowlisted fields (status, versions, durations, units, reason codes), not content. Restrict high-cardinality opaque IDs to access-controlled traces; bounded labels only in metrics. Sample success traces, retain failures with the same redaction, expire diagnostics on a short retention policy. Dashboards: **candidate journey**, **agent quality/cost**, **queues/providers**, **payments**, and **privacy/deletion**.

| Proposed objective | Signal / alert | Immediate operator action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 99.5% monthly successful core read/write requests; p95 submitted text to first useful response <3s | Multi-window error-budget burn or sustained synthetic sign-in/turn failure; distinguish client errors from provider errors. Measure microphone release to first coach audio separately (target <6s excluding user caption approval). | Page for sustained core failure; roll back bad revision, disable unhealthy speech/model route, preserve accepted turns and offer text. Don't fall back to an unevaluated grader. |
| 95% of complete mocks have a report within 2 min | Oldest report >5 min for 10 min; queue depth, lease age, DLQ growth and provider latency by stage. | Prioritize report capacity, pause discovery, check a redacted trace, replay by effect key after the fix. Tell candidates their answers are saved. |
| 99% of enabled daily runs publish an in-app digest within 1h of local due time | Missing run/digest by timezone, feed freshness/coverage, timer heartbeat, mail bounces. One connector failure is not a global outage. | Disable broken connector, publish visibly partial results; backfill missing runs idempotently. Ticket email-only failures when inbox is healthy. |
| Prevent cost/billing surprises | Alert at 80% of daily spend budget; circuit-break optional spend at 100%. Compare reserved/settled usage to provider invoices; ticket webhook lag >10 min or p95 unit cost >2x baseline. | Freeze new optional work, preserve reserved reports, reconcile provider events and ledger. Never grant access based on a redirect. |
| No unauthorized disclosure; live purge within 24h | Isolation canary fails, content in telemetry, deletion backlog >20h, restore drill revives deleted record. | Page immediately, isolate affected path, revoke access, preserve minimal audit evidence, verify scope, follow incident/notification obligations with counsel. |

**Runbook:** on-call acknowledges, records incident/owner and affected cohorts, takes a reversible mitigation, posts a status update without candidate details, then verifies a synthetic end-to-end journey. For an SEV1, target 30-minute update intervals until stable; publish a timeline and corrective owner within two business days. Name a backup responder before selling an availability commitment; an invite-only solo beta should publish support hours rather than promise staffed 24/7 response. Keep kill switches for speech, individual sources, daily drafts, email, model versions and new paid runs.

**Recovery:** enable encrypted Postgres point-in-time restore (30-day retention), protected blob versions/lifecycle where needed, and isolated backups. Reconstruct queues from pending DB operations/outbox; the queue is not the system of record. Same-region recovery targets **RPO <=15 min, RTO <=4h**, subject to a timed restore including blobs and auth. Single-region beta has no automatic regional failover: maintain an approved off-region backup/copy where residency permits, target **24h RPO / 48h RTO** for regional loss, and disclose the wider window. Add zone-redundant HA before a contractual uptime tier. Restore in isolation, reapply an independently retained restricted deletion-suppression ledger before traffic or workers resume, reconcile billing and outstanding work, then run synthetic sign-in, recall, grade and deletion checks. Drill quarterly and before paid GA; a successful backup job isn't evidence of a successful restore.
